### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: continue. The handoff costs 10 minutes of work and the fresh session starts colder. The context estimate may be too high, and the crossing of K may not happen. Everything is on disk, so a compaction would lose little, and the cost gap is only 0.15 USD.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: an estimate from characters, not measured. 30 percent low (80,500) gives C_end 215,500, still over K; cost_continue 1.31.
- T: doubling to 60 widens the fresh side's lead (crossing happens earlier in relative terms). Halving to 15 still crosses K (C_end 182,500).
- F and O: defaults, unmeasured; effort is medium so O may be lower than 1,500. A lower G delays the crossing but does not remove it unless T*G < 65,000.
- B and R: B is the 25,000 default. Higher B shrinks fresh's advantage; R is computed from stated sizes and the project instruction files are not in R (B holds them).
- M (review pauses): 0, no pauses expected. Any miss favours fresh, because the continue context is larger.
- Task type: a refactor with a test oracle is Sonnet work. If it hides a design choice, guardrail 2 would force fresh anyway.
- Prices: table 1 day old, same model both sides, no tier.
- Unrecorded context: none claimed. If some existed, L would add cost to fresh.
