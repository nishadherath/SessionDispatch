### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: continue is the cheaper plan to start, because it avoids writing a handoff and there is no cold start. The margin is 0.15 USD, only just past the 10 percent line, and every input is a default or an estimate (C0 and B are character estimates, T is a plan figure). A fresh session starts colder, and the module's files must be read again. If the compaction point is later than 0.9 W, the compaction penalty (0.075 USD) may never be paid.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: estimated from characters, not measured. If 30 percent low (80,500), C_end is 215,500, still past K, so crossing holds. If 30 percent high (149,500), crossing holds and fresh gets cheaper.
- T: plan estimate of 30. If it doubles to 60, fresh is cheaper still (it crosses K sooner on continue). If it halves to 15, it is under T* (19.6) and continue wins on break-even, but only if the context did not cross K, which it still does (C_end 182,500 against 180,000 is marginal), so this is the one place the verdict is fragile.
- F and O: defaults at medium effort; O was measured at high so it may be lower. Both sides carry the same T*G and T*O terms, so the difference barely moves.
- B and R: B is the 25,000 default, unmeasured; R is by the characters rule. If B is 40,000, C_fresh rises 15,000 and the fresh first read rises, which narrows the margin to about 0.06 USD, inside the band. If R doubles to 40,000, similar narrowing. Instruction files sit in B, not R, so nothing is left out of R.
- M (review pauses): none expected. A pause would raise the miss term on the larger context (continue), which favours fresh.
- Task type: a refactor within one module with its test suite as oracle is row 2. It could hide a design choice if the refactor turns out to change a public interface; none is stated, so not assumed.
- Prices: table 1 day old, no long-context tier on the sonnet row, same model both sides.
- Unrecorded context: none, per the situation. If any exists, L would rise and move toward continue.

Inside the band the quality terms decide (rule 3): continue crosses K, so it loses on quality, and fresh stands. A second stress test, C0 30 percent low (80,500): cost_continue = 1.31 USD against cost_fresh 1.26 USD, 3.5 percent, in the band; quality again gives fresh.

Verdict holds: yes, fragile: the cost margin disappears (falls inside the 10 percent band) at B of about 35,000 or more, or at C0 of about 90,000 or less; the quality term (continue crosses K) keeps fresh in both cases. It flips only if T is under about 17 turns with a context that does not cross K, which is outside the stated range (T = 30).
