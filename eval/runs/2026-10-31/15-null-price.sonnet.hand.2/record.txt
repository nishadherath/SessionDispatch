The opposite verdict, argued: fresh. The window is only 30 percent full,
but C0 of 300,000 is far above B + R of 40,000. A fresh session would
average 107,500 tokens of context against 367,500 for continue, which is
a quarter of the cache-read volume over 30 turns. The handoff is small
(80 lines) and all knowledge is already on disk, so the handoff is cheap
and loses nothing. If the cache-read price is high relative to the
output and cache-write prices, fresh could win on cost.

- C0: measured by `/status`. If 30 percent off (210,000 to 390,000),

  both sides.

  the sign of C0 - B - R.
- M (review pauses): none expected, so 0. Any pause favours fresh.
