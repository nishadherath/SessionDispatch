### Step A: continue or hand off

Turns 1 and 2 (95,950 and 98,950) are at standard prices; turns 3 to 15 (101,950 to 137,950) are at the long-context prices. B alone is 86,450, so nearly every turn pays the tier. This narrows Haiku's advantage over Sonnet, and it is priced below. Sonnet has no tier.

### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: continue on Sonnet. This session holds the reviewed patch and the reasons behind it. A fresh session starts with B of 86,450 and must read another 8,000 tokens cold. A release is outward-facing, and the operator's approval gate may hold the push and tag anyway, so the saving covers only the mechanical part. If the check script fails on reasoning, Haiku would need to escalate, which a Sonnet session already does.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: measured at 400,000. At 30 percent off, continue moves by about 0.18 USD. At C0 = 0, the writes and output alone cost 0.26 USD, above the fresh 0.18, so fresh wins at any C0.
- T: at 30 turns, cost_continue 1.86 and cost_fresh 0.36 (same tier rule). Holds.
- F and O: O at 3,000 (high-effort reasoning): cost_continue 1.29, cost_fresh 0.28. Holds.
- B and R: R at 35,000 (a fresh session reads the whole project), so the fresh start is 121,450 and every turn is long-tier: cost_fresh 0.27 against 0.90. Holds.
- M (review pauses): none expected, M 0. The input that moves the result most is M. At M = 2: cost_continue 2.93 (2 x 422,500 x 2.40/M added to 0.90) and cost_fresh 0.31 (2 x 116,950 x 0.575/M added). Holds.
- Task type: the stage is mechanical only if the check script passes. If it fails on a diagnosis, the stage needs a higher class; that adds a piece and is not priced here.
- Prices: table 1 day old. Ignoring the Haiku tier would lower fresh, so the verdict holds either way.
- Unrecorded context: none claimed. Each unrecorded turn would add about 0.010 USD to the fresh side; five turns give about 0.05 USD, and 0.18 + 0.05 is still well under 0.90.
