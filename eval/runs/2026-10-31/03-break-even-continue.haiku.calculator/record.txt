### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: fresh. Continuing carries 180,000 tokens into a 20-turn stage and pays for an average of 225,000 on every turn. A fresh session starts at 86,450 and reads only the handoff and the module (15,000), averaging about 146,000, so it is the cleaner context. On the break-even arithmetic, fresh becomes the cheaper side once T reaches 36 or more. Against that, fresh costs 1.10 against 0.98 at T 20, and the adverse cases below show how little margin there is in the wrong direction.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: measured. If it is 30 percent high (234,000), T* falls to 21.0 turns, still above T 20, so the verdict holds. The break-even label flips only near C0 of 240,760 (+34 percent), where the two costs are almost equal and the quality rule still gives continue.
- T: a single plan estimate. At 40 turns (calculator run: cost_continue 2.13, cost_fresh 2.09), the margin is 0.04 USD (1.7 percent), inside the band, so the verdict is continue by quality. The cost gap in favour of fresh peaks at about 8.4 percent near 126 turns (my hand calculation from the same formula), under the 10 percent line. No T flips the verdict. The break-even label stops applying from T 36.
- F and O: T* does not depend on F or O, so the short path holds for any values of them. O at medium may be lower than 1,500 (a guess). Lower O moves both sides and leaves T* unchanged.
- B and R: B is measured. R is stated. If R is 30 percent high (19,500), T* is 39.2 turns. If R doubles, T* is 49.7 turns. Both stay above T 20.
- M: unmeasured, default 0, no review pauses expected. Each cache miss costs more on the continue side (225,000 tokens at 2.40 per million, 0.54 USD) than on fresh (146,450 tokens, 0.35 USD). With one miss, continue is 1.515 and fresh 1.448, a gap of 4.6 percent, inside the band, so continue by quality. With two misses, continue is 2.055 and fresh 1.800, so fresh is 14.2 percent cheaper and wins on cost.
- Task type: stated as scoped module work. If it hides a design choice, guardrail 2 matters only across a compaction. Context reaches K only at about 176 turns (C0 + T * G > K), so the verdict holds.
- Prices: the sonnet row is dated 2026-10-30; its cache_write follows the table's rule (1.25 times input). The anthropic family was fetched 2026-10-29. A price shift affects both sides; there is no long-context tier on this row.
- Unrecorded context: stated as none. If 5 turns of rediscovery were needed (L 5), fresh gains 0.20 USD and continue is favoured more. The verdict holds.
