### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: continue costs only 0.15 USD more (11.7 percent), a margin that sits close to the 10 percent band. C0 is an estimate from characters, not a measurement. A handoff is real work and a fresh session starts colder, so on a small margin continuing is the reversible choice. If the unmeasured token counts run lower, the compaction penalty is the only large separator.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: estimated from characters, not measured. If 30 percent lower (80,500) the costs are 1.31 and 1.26 and the margin falls to 3.5 percent, inside the band.
- T: 30 is a plan estimate; at 15 turns the costs are 0.69 and 0.65, margin 6.5 percent; C_end 182,500 still crosses K. Doubling T only raises the gap.
- F and O: defaults, unmeasured, and O assumes high effort (this session is medium, so O may be lower). Lower G shrinks the cost of both sides.
- B and R: B is the 25,000 default and unmeasured; R is estimated from characters. A larger B or R favours continue.
- M (review pauses): 0, none expected; any miss favours fresh, since the cost is on the larger continue context.
- Task type: a refactor within one module with a test oracle is row 2; no hidden design choice stated.
- Prices: table 1 day old; no long-context tier on the sonnet row.
- Unrecorded context: none, everything is on disk (situation statement); L is 0.
