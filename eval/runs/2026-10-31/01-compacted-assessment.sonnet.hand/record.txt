- M: no review pauses expected, so 0. Each miss costs more on the continuing side (235,000 tokens against 190,000).
- Task type: row 5 holds; assessment is design-heavy by the definition in step 1.2.
- Prices: 1 day old; no long-context tier on the fable row.
