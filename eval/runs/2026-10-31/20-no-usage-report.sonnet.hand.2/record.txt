C_cont  = C0 + T * G / 2        = 115,000 + 67,500 = 182,500
C_fresh = B + R + T * G / 2     = 25,000 + 20,000 + 67,500 = 112,500

### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: continue. A handoff is work and a fresh session starts colder. The margin is only 0.15 USD, and every context figure is an estimate with no harness measurement, so the compaction penalty may be smaller than priced. The module's files are already loaded here.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: estimated from characters, harness shows no usage. 30 percent low (80,500): crosses stays true (215,500 > 180,000); the gap narrows to inside the band, quality rule still gives fresh. 30 percent high only widens fresh's lead.
- T: 30 is a plan estimate. Doubled to 60 widens fresh's lead. Halved to 15: crosses stays true (182,500 > 180,000), still fresh. At T of 13 or fewer, crosses is false and the break-even (T* 19.6) says continue.
- F and O (medium effort): defaults, unmeasured. Larger G brings the crossing sooner and favours fresh.
- B and R: B is the 25,000 default, unmeasured. R may omit instruction files. If B + R were 70,000 or more, the gap would close; nothing claims that.
- M (review pauses): 0, none expected. Each miss costs more on the larger continue context, so any pause favours fresh.
- Task type: refactor under a test oracle is row 2; no design choice is hidden in the situation.
- Prices: table 1 day old, no long-context tier on sonnet, all rows present.
- Unrecorded context: none stated; all is in files.
