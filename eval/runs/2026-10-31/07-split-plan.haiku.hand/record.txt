### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: continue on fable at high. The design reasoning lives in the fable session, and the 14 tests encode only part of it, so a sonnet run can misread a contract that the author would have read correctly. Continuing avoids a 10-minute handoff and a 5-minute cold read. The split is cheapest only if the run passes first time: a rerun of the run adds 2.75 USD and takes the split to 7.52, above fresh at 5.62.

- B and R: R at 45,000: fresh 5.85, split 4.89. Verdict holds. The handoff is in both R and H, so R is slightly over-counted (about 2,500 tokens, under 0.02 USD).
- M (review pauses): none expected. At 2 misses: continue 23.67, fresh 7.82, split 5.87. Verdict holds.
- Task type: the design is claimed finished and the tests are the oracle. Nobody claims a hidden design choice, so no change. Risk recorded: a failed run (see opposite verdict).
