### Step B: model, effort and concurrency

Split (step 2.1): the target class is opus, so the third plan is priced. The brief can carry the contract of every changed function, the 14 tests and the observable definition of done (the check script exits 0 and the 14 tests pass), and all of it is on disk. The split is therefore an option, and 4.77 against 5.62 is cheaper than fresh by 0.85 USD, 17.8 percent of the smaller (4.77), which is over 10 percent. The split is the plan, with three pieces.

### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: continue. The design is in this session's head, and a fresh session starts colder. The context is 35 percent of the window and will not cross K. A sonnet run of a cross-module change might miss something the designer would catch, and the acceptance session would then send it back, costing more than it saved. Fresh opus (without the split) avoids the sonnet risk for 0.85 USD more.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: measured. At 30 percent lower (245,000) continue is cheaper but still far above fresh; no flip.
- T: 50 is a plan estimate. At 100 turns no compaction (800,000 < 970,000).
- F and O: defaults, unmeasured, assumed for high effort. A read-heavy stage (F 6,000) raises all three plans similarly.
- B and R: B measured. R (30,000) is from the situation; a bigger module read raises fresh and split alike.
- M (review pauses): none expected, so 0. Any miss costs more on continue (C_cont is twice C_fresh), so misses favour the fresh side.
- Task type: the risk is that the stage hides a design choice. The situation says the design is finished and written down, with contracts and tests; if the brief could not carry it, the split would be a figure only and fresh opus would be the plan.
- Prices: table 1 day old, no long-context tier on the rows used.
- Unrecorded context: none per the situation; if some turned up, H and L rise on the fresh side.

Verdict holds: yes, fragile: the split stops being cheaper than fresh by more than 10 percent at an acceptance of about 19 turns (Ta = 15 is a stated estimate, so 19 is outside the stress-test range only slightly). At Ta of 19 or more the plan falls back to fresh opus (5.62 USD). Doubling T does not flip it. Ta = 30 is a stress test, recorded and not acted on.

### Final verdict

Split, run model claude-sonnet-5-5, effort medium, pieces 3 (serial: brief, run, acceptance by claude-opus-5-5 at high). Human approval step: none, because nothing in the stage leaves the machine; the brief tells the run session not to push. Exact next action: this session commits its work with the gate green, writes the roughly 110-line brief (goal, decisions, design note path, the contract of every changed function, the 14 tests, definition of done, prose rules, five-line escalation) and states the model id and effort on its first lines. It then proposes a card for the operator to start a fresh claude-sonnet-5-5 session at medium effort with that brief, and a second card for the opus acceptance session at high effort after the run commits. This session does not run the stage and does not change its own model.

If the operator wants to override: fresh claude-opus-5-5 at high costs 5.62 USD, 0.85 USD more, with no acceptance session.
