### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: Continuing on Haiku costs 1.12 USD less, about 257 percent less than the fresh Sonnet side. The stage is a refactor with a test suite that checks every change, so a bad step is cheap to detect and redo. Everything the stage needs is on disk, so a handoff loses nothing. Against this, the class rule is not a preference: a refactor run below its matrix class is the error the matrix exists to prevent, and guardrail 8 forbids this session from running it at its current model.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:
- C0: measured at 95,000. At 30 percent off (66,500 or 123,500) the continue side moves by a few tenths of a dollar at most; the class verdict does not move.

The arithmetic says the opposite on cost; flagged for the skill's author.
