### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: continuing keeps the "why" of every decision in context, writes no handoff, and starts nothing cold. The session has not compacted and will not cross K (942,000 of 970,000), so no quality term pushes it out. A fresh session also carries a start-up risk: a missed file in the handoff costs turns.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: measured. If 30 percent lower (420,000), continue is cheaper but still far above fresh (below).
- T: 90 is a plan estimate. If it doubles to 180, C_end passes K and continue gets worse (compaction), so fresh is favoured further. If it halves to 45, continue falls and fresh falls; the gap stays wide.
- F and O: measured; G = 3,800 is measured and below F + O = 6,000. With the default G = 6,000, C_end = 1,140,000 would cross K, which favours fresh. The measured value is the conservative one for fresh.
- B and R: B measured; R is the situation's figure. If R doubles to 40,000, fresh rises by about 0.2 USD. Immaterial.
- M (review pauses): 0, none expected. Any miss costs more on the larger continue context, so misses favour fresh.
- Task type: could it hide a design choice? The situation states scoped work with a stated test. If it were really opus-class, the verdict by class would still be fresh at a higher-class model.
- Prices: table 1 day old; no long-context tier on opus or sonnet rows.
- Unrecorded context: none claimed. Each lost item costs a rediscovery turn (about 0.03 USD at the fresh average); 10 turns of loss would add about 0.3 USD.
