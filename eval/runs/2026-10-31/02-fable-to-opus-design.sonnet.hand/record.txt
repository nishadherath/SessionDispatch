### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: this session holds the reasoning behind the design choices and would not need to read 20,000 tokens cold. The stage is design-heavy and the formula is the centre of the skill, so an Opus session could be argued too low a class; and a handoff costs 10 minutes plus 5 for the read. Continuing avoids both and keeps the "why" in context.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: measured. Reasoning tokens inflate it, which favours fresh. 30 percent lower (177,857) still leaves C0 above B + R.
- T: 60 is a plan estimate; it could reach 90 or fall to 15.
- F and O: defaults, not measured. O assumes high effort; at xhigh it would be larger, raising the Fable side more.
- B and R: B measured; R could be 35,000 if the protocol is read in full.
- M: no review pauses expected, so 0; each pause costs more on the continuing context.
- Task type: could it be Fable (row 5)? It is a revision of one procedure's arithmetic, not a whole-project assessment; acceptance by Fable covers the top-class review.
- Prices: 1 day old; no long-context tier on Fable, Opus or Sonnet.
- Unrecorded context: none named; all on disk per the situation.
