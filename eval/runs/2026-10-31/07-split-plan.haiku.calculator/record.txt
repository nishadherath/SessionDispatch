### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: continue on the current Fable session. The design was made here, so this session holds the reasoning behind it, and a fresh session starts colder, reads 30,000 tokens and repeats some of the decisions. The session is at 35 percent of its window with no compaction ahead, so there is no forced reason to move. Continuing costs 6.72 USD more on the draft figures, but that is 120 percent of the fresh cost, and a fresh start under a written brief loses little because the contract and tests are on disk. The case for continuing is weak on the arithmetic; it rests on warmth, not on a measured loss.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: measured. If 30 percent high (455,000), continue rises to 13.66 USD; if 30 percent low (245,000), continue falls to 11.03 USD. Fresh is unchanged and still cheaper. Holds.
- T: plan estimate with no range. If it doubles to 100, continue is 27.50 USD, fresh 12.79 USD, split 8.36 USD. Split stays cheapest. Holds.
- F and O: estimated. If O is 3,000 (writing-heavy), continue is 17.50 USD and fresh 7.87 USD; the split becomes about 5.90 USD. Fresh beats continue and split beats fresh. Holds.
- B and R: B measured; R from the situation. A larger R moves fresh only, and fresh is already cheaper. Holds.
- M: 0 with no review pauses expected. A pause would add misses on the continue side, which favours fresh. Holds.
- Task type: the design is finished, so the stage is specified. Risk: if the run meets a design choice the brief did not settle, the run is no longer row 2 work and the split is wrong. The run session must stop and escalate to Opus on that, which is the matrix's escalation trigger. Not recomputed as a figure.
- Prices: table 1 day old, no nulls. No long-context tier on these rows. Holds.
- Unrecorded context: none listed. If the design note is incomplete, L grows and the fresh side rises by L times about 0.02 USD per turn of rediscovery at the fresh session's average. Small against the 6.72 USD margin.
