A fresh Opus session also starts colder and needs a handoff written and read, which is work with its own risk of a lost detail. Against this, the acceptance must come from a class above the author, so the cheaper side is not admissible under guardrails 6 and 8, however the figures fall.

- M (review pauses): 0 by the situation. One pause would add a miss on the larger context, which raises continue more than fresh. The class rule still holds.
- Task type: acceptance is row 4 work. If the acceptance decides a merge or release, effort rises to xhigh and the figures are re-run. The situation says it is neither.
