### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: Continuing avoids the 2,500-token handoff, the 25,000-token baseline and the 20,000-token re-read, and it keeps one coherent session. Everything needed is on disk, so the cold-start loss is small. The best case for continue is that the compaction penalty is overstated, but even a zero penalty leaves continue at 1.335 against 1.26, so this case cannot win on cost. It can only win on quality, and step 2 rule 3 sends a session that will cross K to fresh.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: estimated, no usage report. At 30 percent lower (80,500), continue is 1.31 against 1.26, a 3.5 percent margin. That is inside 10 percent, so the quality rule decides, and the session still crosses K, so fresh holds (by quality).
- T: stress test at double (60 turns): continue 3.15, fresh 2.79, margin 12.8 percent. Fresh holds (by cost).
- F: at 5,000 instead of 3,000 (G = 6,500): continue 1.65, fresh 1.50, margin 9.8 percent. Fresh holds (by quality).
- B and R: B at 40,000 gives fresh 1.35 and margin 4.8 percent (fresh holds, by quality). R with the module code at 3.5 chars per token gives R = 22,500, fresh 1.28 and margin 10.5 percent (fresh holds, by cost).
- M (review pauses): none expected. One miss on each side widens the gap, because a miss costs C times 2.4 per million and continue's C is larger. Fresh holds.
- Task type: if the refactor hides a design choice, guardrail 2 applies with crosses true, which gives fresh. Holds.
- Prices: sonnet row, 1 day old, no long-context tier. Holds.
- Unrecorded context (L): if 5 turns of rediscovery were needed, fresh rises by 0.19 to 1.45. Continue (1.41) is then cheaper by 2.8 percent, inside 10 percent, so the quality rule applies: crosses K, so continue loses. Fresh holds (by quality). This is the largest single shift to the fresh side; its value is not claimed by anyone, since the situation says everything is on disk.
- K: assumed 0.9 W = 180,000. Crossing holds for any K below 250,000, so the penalty and the verdict direction do not depend on K's exact value.
