### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: continue. The gap is 0.36 USD, and the compaction penalty is only 0.50 USD, so the money hardly separates the two. A fresh session re-reads 30,000 tokens and depends on a 2,500-token handoff that may miss something the session knows. Continuing needs no operator start click and keeps the working context in place. Against this, guardrail 2 and the quality rule both say a session that has compacted once does not carry a design-heavy stage across a second one.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: measured, approximate. At plus 30 percent (130,000), continue is 12.35 USD, margin 0.81 USD (7.0 percent). At minus 30 percent (70,000), continue is 11.45 USD and cheaper by 0.09 USD (0.8 percent). Both inside 10 percent, so quality decides. Holds.
- T: stated as 60, with no range. At 120, continue 27.35 and fresh 26.31, margin 1.04 USD (3.9 percent). At 30, the margin is 0.4 percent. Holds. Fragile at low T: with no compaction (T of 20 or fewer), continue is cheaper by more than 10 percent, so cost alone would flip the verdict to continue. Guardrail 2 still gives fresh.
- F and O: both sides carry T * G and T * O alike, so the gap does not move. F at 8,000 (the read-heavy default) and O at a higher effort both leave the gap at 0.36 USD. Holds.
- B and R: B is an unmeasured default. At B = 50,000, fresh is 12.23 USD and continue 11.90 USD, so continue is cheaper by 0.33 USD (2.7 percent). Inside 10 percent, quality decides. R includes 9,000 tokens of instruction files that may also sit in B; removing them from R makes fresh cheaper by about 0.25 USD. Holds.
- M (review pauses): none expected. One cache miss adds 2.88 USD to continue (235,000 tokens at 12.25 per million) and 2.33 USD to fresh. A miss favours fresh more. Holds.
- Task type: design-heavy by definition, so guardrail 2 applies. If the stage were mechanical, guardrail 2 would not bind, and the quality rule (compacted loses) still gives fresh. Holds.
- Prices: table one day old, no long-context tier on Fable, no null. A uniform change in price moves both sides in proportion. Holds.
- Unrecorded context: list is empty. At L = 5, fresh rises by 0.89 USD and continue is cheaper by 0.53 USD (4.4 percent). Inside 10 percent, quality decides. Holds.
