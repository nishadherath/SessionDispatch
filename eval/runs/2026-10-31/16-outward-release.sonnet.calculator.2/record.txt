- C0: measured. If 30 percent lower (280,000), continue falls to about 0.65 USD; still above fresh by far.

- B and R: B measured. R is only the handoff and patch; the fresh session also loads the project's instruction files, but B already includes those. R could be 30 percent larger with little effect.

- Task type: the stage is mechanical. The push and tag hide no design choice; the risk is outward, handled by guardrail 3.
- Prices: table 1 day old; the haiku long-context tier is applied to 13 of 15 turns. At tier prices fresh is still far cheaper.
- Unrecorded context: none, as the situation states. Not claimed to be anything else.
