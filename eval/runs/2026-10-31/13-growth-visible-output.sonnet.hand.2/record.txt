### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: continuing keeps the "why" in context and costs no handoff or cold start. Headroom to K is 28,000 tokens, so no compaction is expected, and the 80-line handoff is a small, known piece of work.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: measured. If 30 percent lower (420,000) continue falls, but still far above fresh.
- T: a plan estimate. If it halves to 45, both sides fall roughly in half and fresh keeps its lead. If it doubles to 180, continue crosses K and compacts, which favours fresh further.
- F and O: measured. If G were F + O = 6,000 (reasoning kept in context), C_end would be 1,140,000, over K, so continue would add a compaction penalty. This strengthens fresh.
- B and R: B measured, R from the situation. If R were double (40,000), fresh rises by about 0.05 USD for the first read plus about 0.1 for reads; immaterial.
- M: 0, unmeasured, none expected. Each miss costs more on the larger continue context, so misses favour fresh.
- Task type: the situation says one module, no design choice. If it hid a design choice the class would rise to opus, and the sonnet figure would not stand; nothing in the situation says so.
- Prices: table 1 day old, no tier applies.
- Unrecorded context: none claimed; every item is on disk. If some existed, L turns at the fresh average (about 0.05 USD per turn) would apply, which is small.
