If the stage runs long or a review pause makes the cache miss, the smaller context pays back. Fresh also removes the risk of an unexpected compaction.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: measured. At 30 percent lower (126,000) the gap B + R shrinks to 24,550 and fresh pays off later, so continue is firmer. At 30 percent higher (234,000) T* falls to about 21.6 turns, still above 20.
- T: plan estimate. If it doubles to 40, it exceeds T* (35.5); see the recompute.
- F and O: defaults, unmeasured. They cancel in the short-path T*, so they do not move the break-even.
- B and R: B measured; R from the situation. If R were 30 percent larger, B + R grows and T* rises, which favours continue.
- M (review pauses): none expected, default 0, unmeasured. Each miss favours fresh; the threshold is below.
- Task type: row 2 stands; nothing hints at a hidden design choice.
- Prices: table 1 day old, no tier on the sonnet row, so the average-context method is valid.
- Unrecorded context: none; the situation says all is on disk, so L = 0.
