context, turn t reading C_start + (t - 1/2) * G. The short-path T* does
not apply (tier in play).

```

C_end   = C0 + T * G            = 90,000 + 90,000 = 180,000

C_cont  = C0 + T * G / 2        = 135,000
```

### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: fresh starts with a clean context and its
turns sit at B + R = 96,450, with turn 1 still under the threshold, so
a few more turns pay base prices than the arithmetic of the average
suggests. If C0 were higher or reviews paused the stage, cache misses
would write the larger context again, and a fresh session would carry
the smaller one. Every fact needed is already on disk, so a handoff
loses nothing.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: measured, 90,000. Stress test at 30 percent high (117,000): every continue turn pays the tier, continue 0.2933 against fresh 0.2768, fresh cheaper by 5.9 percent, inside 10 percent, so quality decides and the answer is continue. At 30 percent low (63,000) continue gets cheaper still.
- T: a plan estimate. At 40 turns: continue 0.6044 against fresh 0.6395, continue still cheaper (5.8 percent). C_end is 270,000, still under K. The verdict does not flip.
- F and O: defaults, estimated, medium effort. Both sides use the same G, so a change moves both together; the 4,500 growth per turn is the term that sets the tier crossing, and it is the same on both sides.
- B and R: B measured. R is the situation's 10,000 figure; if R is larger fresh is dearer, so the verdict gets stronger.
- M (review pauses): none expected, default 0. One miss would add 135,000 * 0.575 / 1e6 = 0.0776 to continue and 141,450 * 0.575 / 1e6 = 0.0813 to fresh, both ways about equal.
- Task type: a reviewed list of 40 edits with a check script is mechanical. A hidden design choice would be a reason to go up a class, not to change the session; no one claims one exists.
- Prices: table 1 day old; the haiku tier is applied turn by turn. The handoff write and the first read are priced at base because their context stays under 100,000 (a guess about where the tier boundary falls on those two items; 100,000 would only add under 0.005 USD).
- Unrecorded context: none, L = 0.
