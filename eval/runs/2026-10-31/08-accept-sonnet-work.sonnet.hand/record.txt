### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: continuing saves 1.35 USD (about half the fresh cost), needs no handoff and no operator click, and the session already holds the diff and the reasoning behind it, so it would start with no cold read. Everything needed is on disk, so a handoff loses nothing. But the reviewer would be the author, at the author's class and effort; row 4 says a reviewer at the author's class shares the author's blind spots, and guardrail 6 outranks cost.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: measured, 300,000. 30 percent off moves cost only; class does not depend on it.
- T: 15 is an estimate; doubled to 30 is a stress test (below).
- F and O: estimates (5,000 and 3,000); acceptance at high effort may output more. Larger O raises both sides, fresh more (output 20 vs 10 per million). Class verdict unchanged.
- B and R: B measured. R 30,000 is from the situation; the fresh session may read extra files named in the diff, which raises the fresh side only.
- M: 0, unmeasured; no review pauses expected. A miss would add cost to both sides, more to continue (larger context) but not enough to matter: one miss on continue is 360,000*(2.5-0.1)/1e6 = 0.86 USD, on fresh 176,450*(5-0.2)/1e6 = 0.85 USD.
- Task type: acceptance, not mechanical; no hidden design choice.
- Prices: table 1 day old, no long-context tier on the sonnet or opus rows.
- Unrecorded context: none claimed, so L = 0.
