### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: continue. The costs are within 3 percent, a handoff is work, and a fresh session starts colder. C0 is only half of W and the session holds nothing unrecorded, so a fresh session loses little. The break-even T* of 72 turns exceeds T of 60, so on a pure break-even basis continue would win.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: measured. If 30 percent lower (70,000), cost_continue falls to about 11.45 against fresh 11.54, continue ahead by under 1 percent, still within the band.
- T: estimate. If doubled to 120, both sides roughly double and the stage crosses K more clearly; fresh then gains on cost. Verdict holds.
- F and O: measured on the last assessment; if the assessment is more read-heavy (5,000 to 8,000) the context grows faster and continuing crosses K sooner. Favours fresh.
- B and R: B is a guess (25,000). If B is 45,000, cost_fresh is about 12.09 against continue 11.90, still within the band. R from stated file sizes; nothing obvious left out.
- M: default 0, no review pauses expected. Any miss costs more on the larger continue context, favouring fresh.
- Task type: row 5 holds; it is the design-heavy case by definition.
- Prices: table 1 day old, no long-context tier on the fable row.
- Unrecorded context: none per the situation; if some existed, L would add cost to fresh, but guardrail 2 would still bind.
