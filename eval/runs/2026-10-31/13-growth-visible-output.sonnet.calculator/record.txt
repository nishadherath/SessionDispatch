### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: continuing avoids a handoff, a cold start and any loss of context the files do not capture. The session already holds the module in its head, and it stays under K (942,000 of 970,000), so no compaction occurs. Opus is also stronger if the subcommand turns out harder than planned.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: measured. If 30 percent lower (420,000), C_cont = 591,000 and cost_continue = 17.75 USD; still far above fresh.
- T: a plan estimate. If it doubles to 180, both sides roughly double and continue crosses K; fresh still wins by more.
- F and O: measured over 10 turns. If O were visible-only growth wrong and G = F + O = 6,000, then C_end = 1,140,000 crosses K, C_cont = 870,000 and continue is about 23.76 USD before the compaction penalty; fresh is about 7.75 USD.
- B and R: B is measured. R holds only the handoff and the module; if R doubled to 40,000, cost_fresh rises by under 0.2 USD.
- M (review pauses): none expected. Each miss on continue costs 771,000*(5-0.2)e-6 = 3.70 USD, on fresh 277,450*(2.5-0.1)e-6 = 0.67 USD; misses favour fresh.
- Task type: scoped to one module with tests as the oracle; if it hid a design choice, the class would go up and guardrail 2 would also favour fresh.
- Prices: table 1 day old, derived cache_write is the table's stated rule, no long-context tier on these rows.
- Unrecorded context: none listed; if some existed, L turns at about 0.04 USD each would not change the verdict.
