### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: continue. The gap is only 0.15 USD, inside the range of terms the fresh side omits (the handoff turn's own context read, and the turns spent reading R). A fresh session starts colder, and the handoff is 10 minutes of work plus 5 minutes of reading. Every input about C0 is a character estimate.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: estimated from characters, not measured. At 30 percent lower (80,500) crosses is still true (C_end 215,500 over K 180,000), but the cost gap shrinks to 3.5 percent and the verdict stays fresh, now by quality. At 30 percent higher the gap widens.
- T: 30 is a plan estimate. At 60 turns fresh wins by 12.8 percent. At 19 turns or fewer, the short-path break-even says continue, but C_end would then be about 200,500 and still cross K, so quality still favours fresh.
- F and O: defaults, unmeasured. A larger G makes C_end cross K sooner and strengthens fresh.
- B and R: B is the 25,000 default. R is 80,000 chars / 4, which may undercount if the module files are code. A larger B + R raises T* and narrows fresh's lead.
- M (review pauses): 0 expected. Each miss costs more on the larger continue context, so any miss favours fresh.
- Task type: a refactor within one module with a test oracle. If it hides a design choice, guardrail 2 would force fresh anyway.
- Prices: table 1 day old, sonnet has no long-context tier, so no tier applies.
- Unrecorded context: none, stated by the situation. If some existed, writing it into the handoff would add to H.

It flips to continue only if C_end stays under K 180,000, which needs C0 below 45,000 at T 30, well outside the stated range.
