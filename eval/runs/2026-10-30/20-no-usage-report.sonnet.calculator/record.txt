### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: continue. The fresh side saves only 0.15 USD on a 1.4 USD stage, and a handoff is work: 10 minutes to write and commit, 5 to read, and a colder start. Everything the stage needs is on disk, but a continuing session already holds the module's shape in context. The context estimate is a guess, and if it is high the break-even moves toward continue.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: estimated from characters, not measured. If 30 percent lower (80,500), C_end is 215,500, still above K, so the compaction still happens. If 30 percent higher, continue is worse still.
- T: if it doubles to 60, the context passes K sooner and a compaction is certain; fresh gains. If it halves to 15, T is under T* 19.6 and the stage is nearer the short path, which favours continue (the break-even rule), though 15 turns from C0 115,000 would end at 182,500, barely at K.
- F and O: defaults, unmeasured, medium effort. Larger F or O raises G and pushes the context past K sooner, which favours fresh; smaller G could remove the crossing (T x G under 65,000 means G under 2,167), which is a large cut and unlikely.
- B and R: B is the default 25,000, unmeasured; R is a character estimate. If B + R were 30 percent higher (58,500), fresh costs about 0.1 USD more and the margin shrinks toward the 10 percent line.
- M (review pauses): none expected, so M is 0. Any pause longer than the cache lifetime costs a miss on both sides, and a miss costs more on the larger continue context, which favours fresh.
- Task type: a refactor within one module with tests as oracle is row 2. If it hides a design choice it becomes Opus work, a different decision, and guardrail 2 would then force fresh anyway.
- Prices: table 1 day old, no null on the row, no long-context tier on sonnet. Not a risk.
- Unrecorded context: the list is empty on the situation's word. If something is unrecorded, L rises and fresh costs more by roughly 0.04 USD per rediscovery turn (about 0.0112 + 0.0113 + 0.015 per turn on the fresh side's average context).
