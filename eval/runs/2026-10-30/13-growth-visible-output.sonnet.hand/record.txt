### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: continue. The session holds 600,000 tokens of warm cache and a fresh session starts colder, with a 106,450 token first read and a handoff to write. Opus at high effort is the stronger model, and a sonnet at medium could miss something in a new subcommand. Staying avoids the handoff work and the risk of a lossy transfer. The context is at 60 percent and does not cross K (942,000 against 970,000), so no compaction penalty applies to continuing.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: measured. If 30 percent lower (420,000), C_cont = 591,000 and continue is about 17.20 USD; fresh is unchanged. Fresh still wins by about 170 percent.
- T: plan estimate. If T doubles to 180, C_end = 1,284,000 and crosses is true, which makes continue worse (compaction). If T halves to 45 or falls to 30, see the recomputation below.
- F and O (and the effort O assumes): both measured at high effort in this harness. G = 3,800 is measured and rests on the harness dropping reasoning. If that is wrong and G = F + O = 6,000, C_end = 1,140,000 and crosses is true, which favours fresh more. Fresh at sonnet medium may produce less reasoning output, so O is probably lower than measured (a guess), which favours fresh further.
- B and R: B measured. R is the handoff and the module (20,000, situation figure). If R were 50 percent larger (30,000), cost_fresh rises by about 0.03 + 0.11 USD (first read and cache reads); no effect on the verdict.
- M (review pauses): none expected, M = 0. Any miss adds cost to continue (771,000 * 4.8e-6 = 3.70 USD per miss) more than to fresh (277,450 * 2.4e-6 = 0.67 USD per miss), so misses favour fresh.
- Task type: scoped module work with a stated test. If it hid a design choice, it would move to the opus row and the class rule would favour staying on opus; the situation states one module, no public interface, so this is not indicated.
- Prices: table 1 day old; no long-context tier on these rows. Sonnet cache_write is derived (1.25 times input) per the table note; if it were 20 percent higher, cost_fresh rises by about 0.23 USD; no effect on the verdict.
- Unrecorded context: none per the situation. If some existed, writing it costs output tokens at the opus rate (about 0.02 USD per 1,000 tokens) or L rediscovery turns at the fresh average (about 0.05 USD each at the sonnet rates); small.
