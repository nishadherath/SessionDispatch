### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: continue. The margin is 0.15 USD, which is small against a handoff that costs about 15 minutes of wall clock to write, commit and read, and a fresh session starts colder. Everything the next stage needs is already on disk, so continuing loses nothing, and the compaction penalty is only 0.075 USD. If the harness compacts later than 0.9 W, or not at all, the continue side loses its penalty.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: an estimate from characters, not a measurement. If 30 percent lower (80,500), C_end is 215,500, still above K, and the cost margin falls to 3.5 percent. If 30 percent higher, continue is worse.
- T: if 15 turns, C_end = 182,500, still above K (just), and the margin is 6.5 percent. If 60 turns, continue is worse (more compaction pressure and a larger C_cont).
- F and O (and the effort O assumes): both defaults, unmeasured. G could be lower if the harness drops earlier reasoning; a lower G could stop the crossing. Medium effort keeps O near the default.
- B and R: B is a default (25,000). If the real B is larger, fresh costs more and the margin shrinks. R excludes any instruction files not named in the situation (the situation lists only the handoff and module files).
- M (review pauses): 0 by default; no pauses expected. Each miss costs more on continue (larger context), so any miss favours fresh.
- Task type: refactor within a module with a test oracle is matrix row 2 as stated; a hidden design choice would make it Opus work and make fresh win by class.
- Prices: table 1 day old, sonnet row has no long-context tier, so no tier applies on either side.
- Unrecorded context: none, by the situation's statement. If any exists, writing it raises H, and leaving it out adds L.

Recomputed with the adverse value that moves the result most (C0 lower by 30 percent, 80,500; K and the rest unchanged):

```
C_end         = 80,500 + 135,000 = 215,500 > 180,000, crosses stays true
C_cont        = 80,500 + 67,500 = 148,000
cost_continue = 30*148,000*0.1/M (0.4440) + 0.3375 + 0.4500 + 0.0750 = 1.3065 USD
cost_fresh    = 1.2625 USD (no C0 term)
margin        = 0.044 USD, 3.5 percent of the smaller, within 10 percent
```

Within 10 percent, rule 3 applies: continue crosses K, so it loses on quality; the verdict stays fresh (by quality). A second flip, T = 15, gives cost_continue 0.692 against cost_fresh 0.649 (6.5 percent), crosses true, same outcome.

Verdict holds: yes. The cost margin shrinks under the adverse C0, but the quality rule (the stage crosses K on continue and not on fresh) keeps fresh. No guardrail changed it. If neither C0 nor T supports a crossing (not shown by any case here), the verdict would flip to continue; that is the case to watch.
