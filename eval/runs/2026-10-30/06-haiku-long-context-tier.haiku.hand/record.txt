Continuing carries about 90,000 tokens of earlier conversation into every one of the 20 turns, and on a rename where one missed match is costly, a cleaner context reduces the risk of a wrong edit. The cost gap is 11.5 percent, close to the 10 percent line, so a small change in the inputs could move it. Against that, the fresh session pays for the handoff, the first read and a long-context tier that it crosses sooner.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: measured at 90,000. If it is 30 percent higher (117,000), fresh becomes cheaper by 5.9 percent of its own cost. That is inside the 10 percent line, so the quality rule decides, and it still says continue.

The margin is 9.3 percent, under the line, so the quality rule decides, and it still says continue. This is the adverse value that moves the result most.
- B and R: B is measured at 86,450. R is 10,000 from the situation. If R were 50 percent larger, the fresh side gets dearer, which favours continue. Direction only, not recomputed.
- M: 0, as the situation states. One review pause (one miss) adds about USD 0.0776 to continue and about USD 0.0813 to fresh. The margin becomes 9.9 percent, still inside the line, and continue holds on quality.
