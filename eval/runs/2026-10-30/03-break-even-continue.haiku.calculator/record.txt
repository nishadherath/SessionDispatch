A fresh session would average 146,450 tokens of context against 225,000 when continuing, about 35 percent less, which usually means better recall of detail and less drift across 20 turns. A handoff also forces the oracle and the expected behaviour into writing, which the continuing session has not done. The plan's turn estimate is a guess, and once turns pass T* of 35.5, fresh is the cheaper side. Against that, the cost gap is only 0.122 USD and the verdict rule decides on cost first.
