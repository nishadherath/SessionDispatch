### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: continuing is the reversible choice, needs no handoff, keeps this session's full design context, and avoids the cold start of a new session and the operator clicks to start two of them. The design is finished and 350,000 tokens of it is loaded, so a continuing session might make fewer mistakes on a cross-module change, and a sonnet run can misread the contract and need rework that eats the saving.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: measured (350,000). If 30 percent lower (245,000), continue falls but stays above fresh (see recomputation).
- T: 50 is a plan estimate. If it doubles to 100, all three sides grow; the split still wins by a wide margin (see recomputation).
- F and O (high effort assumed): defaults, unmeasured. If O is double (3,000), every side grows by a similar proportion and continue stays far above the other two, because continue's per-token prices (fable) are 2.5 to 5 times the others.
- B and R: B measured. R is the given 30,000. If R is 30 percent higher (39,000), fresh and split each rise by about 0.05 USD; no change to the verdict.
- Ta and Ra: Ta = 15 and Ra = 40,000 are estimates and the split's lead rests on them. The split loses its 10 percent lead over fresh at about Ta = 19 (computed: split 5.11 against fresh 5.62). If acceptance of a 50-turn change takes 19 turns or more, the plan reverts to fresh at opus high. Flagged as the fragile input.
- M (review pauses): none expected, so 0. Each miss adds more to the bigger context, which favours the fresh and split sides further.
- Task type: the stage is called cross-module, which is opus work, yet the brief carries a full contract and oracle, so it is row 2. If the contract has gaps the sonnet run will hit them. The escalation lines in the brief cover this.
- Prices: table 1 day old; no long-context tier on fable, opus or sonnet rows; none applied.
- Unrecorded context: none per the situation. If one item were missed, L turns would be added to fresh and split: each rediscovery turn costs about 0.04 USD at sonnet.

Verdict holds: yes. Under T doubled, split (8.36) beats fresh (12.79) by 53 percent and continue (27.50) by far. No guardrail changes. The one fragile input is Ta (flips at about 19 turns); noted for the stage report.
