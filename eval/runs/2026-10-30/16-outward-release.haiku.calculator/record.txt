### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: continuing keeps one session with the full context of the patch review and avoids the cost of a handoff and a cold start. Haiku's saving on this stage is 0.72 USD, so the case for continuing is that the saving is small in absolute terms and a fresh session can misapply the patch. The continue side is also the reversible default.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: measured at 400,000. If it is 30 percent lower, continue falls and fresh still wins.
- T: 15 measured as the plan figure. If it doubles to 30 turns with O at 2,000, fresh is 0.48 and continue is 2.28 USD.
- F and O: O is taken from a chore whose effort is not stated. At high effort O may be larger; the O 2,000 case above covers it.
- B and R: B measured. If B is 30 percent higher (112,385) and C0 30 percent lower, fresh is 0.27 and continue is 0.72 USD.
- M (review pauses): none expected. A review pause would add misses to both sides and to fresh's cache writes, which favours continue only slightly.
- Task type: the patch is reviewed and the check script is the oracle, so the row holds. The outward steps are the risk, which guardrail 3 covers.
- Prices: table 1 day old; haiku long-context tier applied to 13 of 15 turns.
- Unrecorded context: none listed.
