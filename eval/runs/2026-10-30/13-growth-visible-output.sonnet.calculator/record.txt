The opposite verdict, argued: continuing avoids writing and committing a handoff, avoids a colder start, and the session already holds the project in context. It stays on opus, which is a higher class than the work needs, and the stage ends at 942,000, just under K, so no compaction.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: measured. If 30 percent lower (420,000), continue costs less but still far above fresh; see recomputation.
- T: if it doubles to 180, C_end passes K, adding a compaction on the continue side only. That favours fresh further.
- F and O (and the effort O assumes): G = 3,800 is measured over 10 turns; O = 3,000 at high effort. If G were 6,000 (F + O, reasoning kept), the calculator gives continue 23.91 and fresh 7.75, so the verdict holds. The fresh session runs at medium, so its O is likely lower; that favours fresh further.
- B and R: B measured. R is 20,000 as stated. If R were 100,000, the fresh side adds about 0.2 for the first read and about 1.0 more on reads; still far below continue.
- M (review pauses): none expected, so 0. Any miss costs more on the larger continue context, which favours fresh.
- Task type: stated as scoped module work with a stated test; no sign of a hidden design choice. If it were design-heavy, the class would rise and fresh would still win by class, but on opus.
- Prices: table 1 day old, no long-context tier for opus or sonnet, so none applied.
- Unrecorded context: none, by the situation's statement. If something were unwritten, each rediscovery turn on the fresh side costs about 0.06 USD at sonnet (C_fresh 277,450 times 0.1 per million, plus G write 0.01, plus O 0.03); even 40 turns would be about 2.4 USD, far under the 14.62 margin.
