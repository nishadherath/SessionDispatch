### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: hand off to a fresh session. A fresh Haiku starts at 96,450 and loses its first-turn tier discount only a turn earlier than continuing, and a clean context keeps 40 mechanical edits free of earlier noise. Doc edits can drift in a context that holds 90,000 tokens of earlier work. If the stage grew past 20 turns the extra context would be priced at the tier either way. None of that wins on the numbers: fresh costs more at every value tried, and the situation says nothing in the context is needed beyond what is on disk.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: measured (90,000). If 30 percent higher (117,000), continue would be at the tier from turn 1, and the gap to fresh would shrink; if lower, continue is cheaper still. A 30 percent error does not make fresh win, since B + R stays above C0 only by a fixed amount.
- T: 20 is a plan estimate. At 40 turns the verdict stays continue (0.60 against 0.64 USD, margin 5.8 percent, so by quality: not compacted, does not cross K).
- F and O (and the effort O assumes): defaults at medium effort, unmeasured, so estimates. At F = 5,000 (read-heavy): continue 0.29, fresh 0.32 USD, margin 10.2 percent, continue by cost.
- B and R: B measured; R is the situation's figure. If R were under-counted (the instruction files are inside B already), fresh gets dearer.
- M (review pauses): none expected, so 0. At M = 2: continue 0.39, fresh 0.43 USD, margin 10.2 percent, continue.
- Task type: a rename of a key across 40 reviewed edits with a check script is row 1. A hidden design choice would be a rule change, but the list is already reviewed. Haiku holds; if the check script failed on reasoning, the escalation trigger (not a preference) would apply.
- Prices: table 1 day old; the long-context tier applied to 18 of 20 turns (continue) and 19 of 20 (fresh).
- Unrecorded context: none, so L = 0. Each turn of rediscovery would cost more on the fresh side only.

Recomputed with the adverse value that moves the result most (T = 40, then F = 5,000, then M = 2):

Verdict holds: yes. Continue wins in every case; no value crosses to fresh and no guardrail binds.

### Final verdict

continue, model claude-haiku-5-5, effort medium, pieces 1 (serial). Exact next action: apply the 40 reviewed edits in order from the list on disk, run the project's check script after each batch, commit locally when it exits zero, and do not push. The session has the model and effort the plan names, so no switch is needed. Nothing is asked of the operator; no approval step is needed because nothing leaves the machine.

Cost of the stage, continue: 0.25 USD at the tier (Haiku's advantage over Sonnet narrows here: the tier lifts its cache-read rate to 0.05 per million, but it is still cheaper than Sonnet).
