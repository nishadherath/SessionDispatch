The opposite verdict, argued: continuing keeps all the design reasoning in a session that already holds it, avoids a handoff and a colder start, and fable at xhigh is the stronger designer on a stage whose choices are the product. The context is only 25 percent of the window and will not compact, so the usual quality penalty for fresh does not apply the other way. Everything needed is on disk, though, so continuing buys little that a fresh session cannot read in 20,000 tokens.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: measured. 30 percent lower (178,000) cuts C_cont to about 358,000 and continue to about 17.9 USD, still far above fresh.
- T: 60 is an estimate. At 120 the context crosses K (C_end 974,081), continue is about 45.80 USD including the compaction penalty, fresh about 22.65 USD. Fresh holds. At T 30, continue is about 10.7 USD and fresh about 4.9 USD; holds.
- F and O: O at xhigh may be 4,500 (estimate). Then continue 26.31 USD and fresh 12.28 USD. Holds. The fable-side output is the main driver of the gap.
- B and R: B measured; R is the stated sum. If R missed 20,000 tokens, fresh rises by about 0.15 USD. Immaterial.
- M (review pauses): none expected. A miss costs more on the larger continue context, so any pause favours fresh further.
- Task type: row 3 (opus, high) is the lower bound for this stage. Treating it as row 5 (fable) would be a class raise with no measured failure, which the skill forbids.
- Prices: table 1 day old, no long-context tier applies to these models, ttl 5m measured. No change.
- Unrecorded context: none claimed (stated that it is all on disk). If some existed, each item adds handoff output, about 0.05 USD per 1,000 tokens, far below the margin.
