### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: continue. It is 1.48 USD (41 percent) cheaper, T* is 188.5 turns, there is no compaction risk and nothing is unrecorded, so the handoff is overhead. Counter: the author reviewing its own model shares its blind spots, and it cannot raise its own effort. Those are rules, not costs.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: measured. At 30 percent lower (105,000) T* rises, continue gets cheaper still; at 30 percent higher (195,000) T* falls to about 94 turns, still far above 12.
- T: stated as 12. Doubled to 24, continue 7.48 and fresh 8.86 USD, the draft side holds on cost.
- F and O: estimates. xhigh may lengthen reasoning (the skill says test a larger O). O of 4,500 changes both sides by the same T * O * p_out, so the gap is unchanged.
- B and R: B measured; R estimated at 30,000 from the situation. If R were 60,000, T* is about 640 turns and the draft side is unchanged.
- M: 0 by default, no pauses expected. Each miss costs more on the larger side (continue 198,000 and fresh 164,450 tokens at 12.25 USD per million extra), so misses would favour fresh slightly: three misses make the sides about 3.59 + 7.28 = 10.9 USD and 5.07 + 6.04 = 11.1 USD.
- Task type: acceptance, row 4. It hides no mechanical step.
- Prices: table 1 day old, no long-context tier for fable, ttl 5m measured. If ttl were 1h the table has no `cache_write_1h` for fable, which would stop the calculation; not the case here.
- Unrecorded context: none. If something is unrecorded, L rises and fresh gets dearer, but guardrail 6 still holds.
