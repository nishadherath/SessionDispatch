The stage ends with a clean context of 163,450 tokens average instead of 137,000, and the spec read is the same either way. A fresh run also leaves this session's 110,000-token context out of every later turn. The cost gap widens as T grows.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: measured. At 30 percent higher (143,000) continue costs more and fresh is unchanged; at 30 percent lower continue gets cheaper. The cost line is crossed on the high side.
- T: 12 is a plan estimate, not a measurement. At 24 turns (doubled) the calculator gives continue 2.05 USD against fresh 1.48 USD, 38.5 percent apart, so fresh wins by cost. By my linear estimate (not run through the calculator, as the shell was withheld) the 10 percent line is crossed near T of 13.

- M (review pauses): 0, none expected. Each miss costs more on the larger continue context and would favour fresh.
- Task type: row 2 on the situation's description; no hidden design choice, since the spec states the outputs.
- Prices: table 1 day old; no long-context tier on these rows; ttl 5m measured.
