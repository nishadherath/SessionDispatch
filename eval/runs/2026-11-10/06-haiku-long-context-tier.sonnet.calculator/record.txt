The opposite verdict, argued: a fresh session starts at 96,450 against 90,000 now, so it carries 6,450 more tokens, but it stays under the 100,000 tier for one turn rather than two and its context is clean. The tier is what makes both sides expensive, and a cheaper handoff could shave the gap. Fresh is only 0.029 USD dearer, and the fresh side's omitted terms could swing that either way. Against this: B + R is above C0, so a fresh session is larger from turn 1 and never recovers the handoff cost.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: measured. At 30 percent lower (63,000) the continue side gets cheaper still (more turns under the tier); at 30 percent higher both sides are in the tier throughout and the gap stays near the handoff and first-read cost.
- T: estimate. At 40 turns the margin shrinks to 5.8 percent (within the band), so quality decides: not compacted, no crossing (C_end 270,000), so continue.
- F and O: defaults, unmeasured. They move both sides together; the continue side keeps its lead because it starts smaller.
- B and R: B is measured. R is stated; a larger R only hurts the fresh side. If R were 0, fresh would start at 86,450 and could beat continue by a small margin; nobody claims R is 0, because the handoff and edit list are 10,000.
- M: none expected. One miss would add to the larger fresh context more than to continue; the verdict holds.
- Task type: a rename with a reviewed list and a check script is row 1. The risk is a hidden design choice, but the list is reviewed.
- Prices: table 1 day old; the tier is applied turn by turn on both sides. Priced at the 5-minute write as measured.
