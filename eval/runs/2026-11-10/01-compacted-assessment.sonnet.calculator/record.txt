### Adversarial pass

The opposite verdict, argued: continue. The costs are almost equal (11.90 against 11.54 USD, 3.1 percent), so fresh saves under half a dollar, and the handoff is work that starts a colder session. The session holds the "why" of the project and everything is on disk anyway. A compaction costs only 0.50 USD in the model, and the model changes nothing about class or effort.

Each input, the way it could be wrong:

- C0: "about 100,000". If 30 percent lower (70,000), C_end is 340,000 and still crosses K; continue gets cheaper, fresh does not. Cost could favour continue by a wider margin, but quality (guardrail 2) still decides.
- T: 60 is a plan estimate. At 30 turns the session still crosses K (235,000); by hand continue 5.69 USD, fresh 5.67 USD (0.4 percent), a tie that goes to the quality rule: fresh. At 120 turns the context passes W many times over and compactions multiply, which favours fresh further.
- F and O: measured on the previous assessment at high effort. If F doubles to 6,000 the growth and the crossing get worse for continue; the effort assumed is high, so no stress from longer reasoning.
- B and R: B 25,000 is an unmeasured default. A B of 40,000 raises fresh by about 0.2 USD (first read plus the average-context reads), which does not change the quality verdict. R is listed in full from the situation; nothing left out.
- M (review pauses): none expected, none measured. With M = 3 by hand, continue adds 3 x 235,000 x 12.25 per million = 8.64 USD and fresh adds 3 x 190,000 x 12.25 per million = 6.98 USD, so fresh would lead by about 11 percent. Pauses favour fresh.
- Task type: really an assessment and roadmap, so design-heavy; no hidden mechanical part that would permit a lower class.
- Prices: table 1 day old; no long-context tier on the fable rows; 5m write price used because the usage report says so. At the 1h write price (not claimed) writes would cost more on both sides and move T* out.
- Unrecorded context: none, by the situation's statement. If something were unrecorded it would be added to the handoff or to L, which raises fresh.
